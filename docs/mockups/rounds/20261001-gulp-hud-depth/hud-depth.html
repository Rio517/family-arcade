Gulp Universe: controls with depth

In a round, the controls are flat: single-colour pills, dark glass or plain white, with no light on them and one hard shadow at most. Four ways to make them look like pieces of the toy city. All four also give the pieces that stay up all round less room (the leaderboard most), drop the star and hole counters, and show the points for swallowing a hole.

Keys: 1–5 pick a look, ← → step through them, S shows them all side by side.
