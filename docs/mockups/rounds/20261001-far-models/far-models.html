Simpler buildings from high up

Late in a round, the drawing work sets the frame rate, not the game's own code. From about level 15 the camera is high enough that the city could be drawn with plainer copies of its buildings: the same shapes and colours, with fewer facets. What changes and what stays

Changes

  • Rounded corners take two steps instead of three or more.
  • Top edges of walls and roofs are square instead of softly rounded.
  • Small round windows and skylights get fewer sides: hexagons and octagons.
  • Round things like helipads and domes have fewer sides, never fewer than ten.
  • Trees and bushes are blockier.
  • Parts under 0.4 units every way are left out: door knobs, the flower by a house's door.

Stays

  • Every building's outline, size and colours.
  • Windows, doors, roofs, chimneys, signs and helipads.
  • Everything below level 15: the simpler models are used only from high up.
  • Shadows: they come from the same models, so they get cheaper too.
