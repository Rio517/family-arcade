Gulp on a phone: room for the scores

On a phone, the leaderboard tray is 140px wide and shares the top row with the clock, the level meter and the buttons. Most phones don't have room for all of that, and four-digit scores don't fit in the tray. Each option below gives the tray room for a full score and moves one other piece. Nothing changes on iPad.

Today, in a real round on a 393px phone (iPhone 15 size)

  • The clock and the level meter sit on top of the tray, so the scores are hidden from the start of the round.
  • Score digits are square pixels, so "9,999" is 67.5px wide. The tray has 61px left for the name and the score together, and every score of 1,000 or more is cut off, at every difficulty. Medium rounds end at about 4,000 to 5,000.
  • Two power-ups running overlap the level meter at 375 and 393px.
  • Only the 430px phone clears the clock, by 3px, and its scores are still cut off.
