CHESS 3D · GALAXY · WAR ROOM · CLOUD KINGDOM

👤 1–2 players📱 one iPad or two⏱ ~15 min🤝 party-ready

Real chess with three worlds. Play on one iPad passing it back and forth, or against a friend on their own — the party seats you both, no code. Free play lets you set up any puzzle.

What it is. Every ticket gets a small Peek. Tap it and a sheet slides up: one real screenshot, the name, the facts (players, devices, how long, party-ready), a two-line "what it is", and a big Play. Tap anywhere else to drop it. The ticket itself still plays on tap, exactly as today.
Why it works. Nothing changes on the landing page until you ask; the sheet is the one place to "sell" a game, and it reads on a phone. Kids get one big picture and one big button.
Cost. One screenshot per game bundled as a small webp (~40–60 KB each, ~400 KB total, offline-safe), a preview block on each game descriptor (facts + blurb), one shared sheet component. About a day, plus the screenshot curation.

What it is. The chevron on each ticket becomes "open": tap the ticket and it grows in place — a wide screenshot strip, the facts, the blurb, a Play button. Tap the header again (or another ticket) and it folds back. No sheet, no dimming.
Why it works. Everything stays on one scrolling page; you can open two tickets and compare. It feels like the ticket is the poster.
Cost. Same bundled screenshots and descriptor block as A, plus the ticket needs an open/closed state and a "Play" that's separate from "open" (today the whole card is the Play button — that's the one real UX change, and on a phone a tall open ticket pushes the others below the fold). What it is. Every ticket carries a little moving poster — a slow pan across a screenshot with a shimmer (a real looping clip later, if ever). The landing page becomes an arcade cabinet row: you see all the games moving before you touch anything. Tapping still plays.
Why it works. Maximum "see it" with zero taps; it looks alive. The Midnight Carnival was built for this.
Cost. Same screenshots, but every ticket now shows one — the landing page loads 7 images and animates them (fine on iPad, watch older phones; reduced-motion gets stills). Real video loops would be 1–3 MB each, which the offline bundle can't afford. And it does not answer "who is it for / how long" — you still need A or B on top for the facts. Two days for the pan-and-shimmer version.
