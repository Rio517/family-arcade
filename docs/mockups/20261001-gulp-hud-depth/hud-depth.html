Gulp Universe: controls with depth

In a round, the controls are flat: single-colour pills, dark glass or plain white, with no light on them and one hard shadow at most. Three ways to make them look like pieces of the toy city. Positions, sizes, words and the colours that mean something stay as they are.

Keys: 1–4 pick a look, ← → step through them, S shows all four side by side.
