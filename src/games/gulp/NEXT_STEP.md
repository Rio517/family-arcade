# Gulp Universe: open feedback

What is still open for Gulp Universe. Finished items come off this list;
`git log` holds what shipped.

Status: **todo**, or **decide** (needs the owner).

| Item | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 4 lasts too long | todo | A steady player sits at level 4 for about 140 s of a City round. Soften that step, or add something worth growing into. |
| The rivals' leaderboard feels flat next to Gulp City's | todo | Gulp City's rivals climb to 35–60k points in 2 minutes; ours stay under 3k in 4. A late-round surge for show, without making Easy harder. |
| Finish the fun audit | todo | A pass through every map for anything dull, confusing or broken for a young player. |
| Load the menu's city in stages | decide | The menu's city takes about 0.6 s to build on a slow CPU. Building the nearest blocks first shortens that freeze, but far blocks pop in. |
| Performance guards in the tests, and a Lighthouse check in CI | decide | Caps on triangles per map, model reuse between menu and round, and model build work; optionally a Lighthouse budget on pull requests. |
| A best-scores history | decide | Best scores are kept per player, map and level; a list of past rounds or a family leaderboard would need its own screen. |
