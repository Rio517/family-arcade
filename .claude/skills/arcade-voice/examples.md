# Arcade voice: before and after

Lines from the arcade's voice pass. "State" is
the capitals line; "sub" is the plain sentence under it. A wink, where there is one, is marked.

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Front door | ★ Step right up to the ★ Family Arcade · insert coin · pick a game | State: ★ FAMILY ARCADE ★ · Sub: Pick a game. Free play, all night. *(wink)* |
| New player | Step right up! Make your ticket — it keeps your points and wins in every game. | State: NEW PLAYER · Sub: Make your ticket. It keeps your points and wins in every game. |
| Game card, Gulp | Be a hungry hole: swallow the city, grow bigger and bigger, and out-eat the computer holes. | You're a hungry hole. Swallow the city and grow bigger than the computer holes. |
| Warning, Gulp | Look out! A fuel truck! / Swerve out of its way | State: FUEL TRUCK! · Sub: Swerve out of its way. |
| Close loss, racer | {name} wins! / So close — you got {n} coins. | State: {NAME} WINS · Sub: You got {n} coins. The crowd wants a rematch. *(wink)* |
| Crash | Uh oh — that game hit a glitch! / Nothing is lost. Head back to the arcade and try again. | State: OUT OF ORDER · Sub: A gremlin got in. Nothing is lost: go back and try again. *(wink)* |
| Waiting, play together | The other iPad has not come in. Is it awake? | State: WAITING FOR PLAYER 2 · Sub: Is the other iPad awake? Give it a tap. |

## Why these changed

- **States became capitals of four words or fewer**, so the eye gets the situation in one glance;
  the sentence under it carries the rest.
- **Three winks in seven screens** (front door, close loss, crash), each in a subline you can skip.
  The warning and waiting screens stay plain because the player is about to act.
- **Buttons are not shown here:** they stay verb-first, three words ("Race again", "Back to arcade").
